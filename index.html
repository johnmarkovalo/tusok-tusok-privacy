Privacy Policy: Tusok! Tusok!

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Developer: Symflo IT Solutions, Philippines

Contact: support@symflo.dev

Tusok! Tusok! ("the game") is a single-player game. We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and the game has no accounts or chat.

What stays on your device

  • Your save (day, cash, what you've bought, your suki and story progress) and your settings are stored on your device only.
  • The playtest log. The game keeps a short log of how each day went (scores, customers served, purchases) on your device. It is never sent anywhere by the game. If you choose Kopyahin ang log in the settings, it is copied to your clipboard so you can send it to us yourself.

Deleting the game deletes all of this.

Advertising

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising software, so it doesn't use your device's advertising ID. If a future version adds ads, we'll update this policy before that version is released.

Crash reports

If the game crashes or freezes, it may send a crash report to Sentry so we can fix it. A report contains the error, the game version, your device model and operating system version. It does not include your name, contacts or location, and we don't use it for advertising.

Sentry's privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the policy at this address.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@symflo.dev
